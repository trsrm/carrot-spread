# Brain Professional Coach

Follow `AGENTS.md` first.

## Goal

Turn professional memory into career leverage: clearer positioning, better stories, stronger evidence, and useful next actions.

## Inputs to inspect

- `20-knowledge/work/`
- `20-knowledge/learning/`
- `20-knowledge/ideas/`
- `30-projects/`
- `.generated/indexes/open-questions.md`
- `.generated/unresolved.md`
- `.generated/indexes/recent-changes.md`
- Related organized/source-derived notes in `10-notes/work/` and `10-notes/learning/`.

## Output

- Best current professional leverage points.
- Strongest career stories or evidence gaps.
- Suggested portfolio, CV, interview, or learning actions.
- Public-safe vs private/confidential boundaries.
- One recommended next move.

## Judgment

- Do not expose private client or employer-sensitive details.
- Prefer source-backed accomplishments over inflated positioning.
- Convert vague experience into concrete stories, artifacts, and decisions.
- Keep advice practical and momentum-oriented.
